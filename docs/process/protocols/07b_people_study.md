# Protocol 07b — Priority 7 (people part): can people outside the team act on the explanations alone?

Registered 2026-10-14, before any material is shown to anyone. Frame: protocol 00. Run by the team,
which recruits the participants. Pack: `docs/process/people_study/`.

## Question
Reading only SHAHID's explanation text, do people outside the team (a) infer the cause from the
evidence numbers and (b) choose the action the escalation table intends?

## Participants
At least 8 people outside the team, adults, any background; recruited by the team. Anonymous: only
a participant code (P01, P02, …) and one background category (electrical engineering / other
engineering / other) are recorded. No names, no contact details. Verbal consent with the text in
`INSTRUCTIONS.md`. Nobody who has seen SHAHID's code, documents or demo takes part.

## Material (generated, not hand-written: `python -m shahid.p7_people booklets`)
- 12 explanations, verbatim from `shahid.explain`, from the stored demo replays (`results/demo`,
  SIMULATED): 4 outage verdicts, 4 communication-failure verdicts, 3 abstentions and the
  confident-wrong 10:00 verdict of `feeder_outage` (finding 09). Selection is fixed by a seeded draw
  (seed 7) within each class, from groups of at least 2 meters.
- **Form B (evidence only)**: the sentences that state the conclusion ("most likely lost power /
  still have power", "Confidence in this cause", "The alternative considered") are removed; the
  evidence numbers stay.
- **Form A (full text)**: the explanation as the operator sees it.
- Two booklets: booklet 1 shows items 1–6 in Form B and items 7–12 in Form A; booklet 2 the reverse.
  Participants alternate between booklets by code order. No item is seen in both forms.

## Answers (anonymous answer sheet, one row per item)
- Form B: cause (outage / communication failure / cannot tell) and confidence 1–5.
- Form A: action (send a repair crew / repair the data link, no crew / ask a supervisor or wait for
  more data) and clarity 1–5.
- Optional: which number made you decide (free text, no personal content).

## Analysis (`python -m shahid.p7_people analyse`)
- Intended answer: OUTAGE → crew; COMMS_FAILURE → data link; abstention → supervisor or wait. Form B
  cause: the system's verdict ("cannot tell" for abstentions).
- **H7a**: Form B agreement with the system's cause, over the 11 items excluding the wrong one,
  pooled over participants: Wilson 95% interval; supported if the lower bound exceeds 1/3 (chance
  among three options).
- **H7b**: Form A agreement with the intended action, same rule.
- Fleiss' kappa among participants per form. Per-item agreement table.
- **Confident-wrong item** reported separately: share following the system (an over-trust
  indicator, not a success measure).
- Fewer than 8 participants → reported as underpowered; no hypothesis is marked supported.

## Effort ESTIMATE
About 20 minutes per participant; analysis minutes.
