# CP11 — Resolution gate, number integrity and proposal pack

> **Superseded in part by CP12.** "Version 1.1" bundles two rules that CP12 reports separately, and CP12 recomputes the attention numbers under one matching rule. Where they differ, CP12 and its table of changed statements apply.

Version 1 (detector, thresholds, simulation, CP7 results) is unchanged and remains the registered
result. Version 1.1 is a post-processing and escalation layer on version 1 outputs. Every version
1.1 number is a "post-hoc variant, not pre-registered", selected under a declared deviation
(`docs/eval_protocol.md` addenda B and C). Sources: `results/cp11_select.json`,
`results/cp11_eval.json`, ledger CP11-*.

## Implemented
- `shahid/cp11_sims.py`: per-group timing (onset reading recovered, meters heard again) and Isolation Forest verdicts on 25 record families. Observation re-simulation only; worlds are cached and only outage rows are re-solved. At most 2 workers; runtime is logged per family in `results/cp11_sims.log` and `results/cp11_extra.log`.
- `shahid/cp11.py`: the version 1.1 policy (gate → WATCH with timeout; provisional comms re-check, i.e. revised R3), the gate sweep with the committed rule (`select`), and the evaluation (`evaluate`): operator load for every method, whole-feeder outcome tables, Isolation Forest AUC with interval, pooled fault-free bounds, outage attention and the untouched grid.
- Addendum B (gate rule, before any version 1.1 number) and addendum C (declared deviation, development data only, before any held-out version 1.1 number). Decision `docs/process/decisions/0004-v11-resolution-gate.md`.
- `shahid/check_numbers.py` + `tests/test_numbers.py` + `docs/number_allowlist.yaml`: a two-level number-integrity chain (documents → ledger or cited result files; ledger → cited result files).
- `shahid/make_docs.py`, `shahid/make_pack.py`: `docs/RESULTS.md` and `docs/process/proposal_pack.md` are now generated from result files, with six 300-dpi figures `results/figs/pack_*.png`.
- `shahid/reproduce.py` includes every CP10 and CP11 stage. `python -m shahid.verify_repro --all` is the one-command unattended check. The README lists what has and has not been verified in a fresh clone.

## Measured
- **Step 1 (what arrives after an event), from `shahid/observe.py`:** after a comms failure the stored readings arrive after the link returns (backlog on by default). After an outage the unpowered intervals never arrive (backfill off by default), and the same holds for random loss. In the simulation a comms failure is therefore distinguishable from data alone once data resumes, but only under assumption A-COMMS. A random loss and a brief single-meter outage stay indistinguishable.
- **Task 1 (CP11-T1a–c):** on operator load, version 1 is **worse** than the step, count and structure baselines and Isolation Forest. Those rarely abstain, so they send fewer items to a human but make many false or missed outages. It is about **equal** to the naive baseline. Whole-feeder outcome tables are in `docs/RESULTS.md` §4. Isolation Forest cause attribution: AUC 0.411 (95% CI 0.341–0.484), anomaly score as predicted outage, CP5 validation. The interval lies below 0.5; this is no evidence of any cause attribution and is not presented as such.
- **Task 2, selection (CP11-T2a):** under addendum B's rule no setting is feasible, because version 1 itself leaves 0.119 of validation branch and feeder outages without real-time attention. Under addendum C's incremental reading, exactly one setting is feasible: gate groups of fewer than 3 meters, timeout 1 h.
- **Task 2, evaluation (CP11-T2b–f):** pooled fault-free false outage verdicts per transformer-year fall from 32.7 to 0.2 (semiurb5) and from 33.3 to 0.4 (urban6). Human items per transformer-day on held-out event data fall from 11.76 to 0.99. Outages without real-time attention rise from 0.138 to 0.156 (held-out), 0.083 to 0.126 (whole-feeder, held-out) and 0.091 to 0.212 (untouched rural3). The revised R3 re-check catches 51 of the 57 outages that version 1 auto-closed under 10 kW load-shaped unmetered energy, and 11 of 11 under 2% transformer gain error.
- **Task 2 Step 7 (untouched grid rural3, run once, CP11-T2f):** frozen version 1 has coverage 0.494, accuracy when deciding 1.000, no false and no missed outages. The naive baseline makes false outages at 0.173. Version 1 reaches an accuracy of 0.99 or more again, as in CP7. It runs the same code that passed the CP7 leak audit, and the figure is reported only with its coverage.
- **Task 3 (CP11-T3):** before fixes, 906 document numbers were checked with 155 unmatched against the ledger, and 264 ledger numbers with 20 unmatched against their cited files. None contradicted its source. Two ledger values (C2-01) are not backed by any existing file, and are now marked "do not report". After fixes and generation, 1 796 document numbers and 397 ledger numbers have 0 unmatched outside the documented allowlist.

## Failed or surprising
- **The committed selection rule was infeasible by construction.** Its real-time-attention limit (2% absolute) is violated by version 1 itself, because short simulated outages end before any decision. The registered outcome is therefore "version 1 alone". Version 1.1 exists only under a declared deviation.
- **The selection objective missed a cost.** Human items were counted on fault-free data only. On event data, the 1-hour comms re-check escalates most genuine comms failures, because they last longer than an hour and the stored readings arrive only afterwards. The net load is still far lower than version 1's, and this is reported, not re-tuned.
- **Gating on SNR or expected energy does not remove false outage verdicts.** Those sit on 1–2-meter groups with high apparent SNR, and only a meter-count gate removes them.
- **The gate's cost is largest on the untouched rural grid**: outages without real-time attention rise from 0.091 to 0.212.
- **Attention matching is by onset only.** It assigns any silent group starting at an event's onset to that event. Evaluation matching uses a set overlap of at least 0.5. Counts for simultaneous events therefore differ from CP10's R3 figures.
- **Runtimes:** the CP11 record families with the untouched grid took 967 s (`results/cp11_extra.log`). The other families took 15–133 s each, at most 2 at a time.

## Assumptions remaining
- A-COMMS (meters store and resend readings) now carries the version 1.1 re-check. Without a backlog, the re-check escalates every comms failure at the timeout.
- New DESIGN CHOICES: the version 1.1 gate (n < 3, T = 1 h) and the incremental attention limit (addendum C). Neither is operator-validated.

## Decision and recommendation
- **Registered result: version 1 alone** (addendum B outcome).
- **Version 1.1 as a clearly labelled post-hoc option.** It cuts false outage verdicts by about two orders of magnitude and operator load by about ten times on held-out data with events. Its cost: more outages go without real-time attention, especially single connections and the rural grid. The team should present it as a configurable policy with this trade-off, not as a validated improvement.
