# Phase 2 candidates (from CP10; NOT implemented, version 1 is frozen)

Every item comes from a post-hoc descriptive analysis added after CP7 (`results/cp10.json`). None
has been tested as a method change. A change must go through the full protocol again: develop on
training and validation weeks, freeze, then evaluate on new held-out data.

| # | Candidate | Evidence | Expected effect | Risk |
|---|-----------|----------|-----------------|------|
| 1 | **Do not route single-meter "uncertain" items to a human immediately**: wait for persistence (e.g. several hours of silence) or require a minimum group size before escalating | At 1% packet loss the detector produces about 10–11 uncertain items per transformer-day, over 99% of them from 1–2-meter silences. At 10% loss it is about 66–68 per day (CP10-T1) | removes most operator load | a genuine single-connection outage waits longer for a human |
| 2 | **Size-aware prior or minimum size for OUTAGE verdicts** | Fault-free false outage verdicts are 32–40 per transformer-year, all on 1–3-meter silent sets (CP10-T2) | cuts false outage verdicts and improves P(real outage given an outage verdict) at realistic outage frequencies | misses single-connection outages, which are already almost always abstained |
| 3 | **Load-dependent gap baseline** (gap = a + b × reporting load, fitted on recent history) | Transformer-meter gain error and load-shaped unmetered energy both make the gap residual correlate with load change (−0.71 and 0.56). A load-dependent fit cuts residual spread by 26% and 21% (nominal: 2%) (CP10-T8) | lowers missed outages under both failure modes | must be re-validated; the residual spread stays 6–16× nominal |
| 4 | **Decide on signal-to-noise, not on silent share**: report and gate decisions on expected silent energy divided by its predictive sd | The 90%-correct point is most consistent on this axis: sd of log10 across 29 cells 0.14, against 0.20 for share and 0.44 for kWh; held-out/dev ratio 0.81 (CP10-T3) | a portable boundary across grids | needs calibrated predictive sd |
| 5 | **Split simultaneous silent sets into electrical zones** and test zone combinations | 37% of outages missed when an outage and a comms failure start together (C6-06) | removes the merged-group failure | combinatorics on scattered comms sets |
| 6 | **Wait for late readings before automated comms closure** (rule R3 is now provisional with an 8-hour backlog re-check, a DESIGN CHOICE) | R3 closed 0.9–1.8% of real outages without a human nominally, and up to 24% under load-shaped unmetered energy (CP10-T0) | catches confident wrong comms verdicts | depends on meters storing and back-filling readings (A-COMMS) |
| 7 | **Calibrate the diversity model** against real household data (licence to be checked) | Coverage ranges 0.48–0.66 over the swept diversity values (CP10-T7) | turns a range into an estimate | — |
| 8 | **Size-dependent prior instead of 50/50** (refines #2) | CP12 Task 3: single-meter residuals are not under-dispersed (90% interval holds 0.95; deep lower tail lighter than the Student-t model); false outage verdicts occur at about the model's own tail rate, so their count follows the number of tiny groups (base rate), not a variance error | removes most false outage verdicts without touching the uncertainty model | the prior must come from real outage and data-loss frequencies, which are not available here |
| 9 | **Escalate, do not close, a WATCH group whose meters resume without the onset reading** | CP12 Task 1: under A-COMMS this is the signature of an outage, yet the gate closes it with no verdict; 16–17 of 350 whole-feeder outages per grid are lost this way | recovers the gate's lost small-feeder outages | more human items from random loss of the onset reading |
| 10 | **Measure real communication-failure durations before choosing a re-check timeout** | CP12 Task 2: at 1 h the re-check sends 0.54–0.81 of genuine comms failures (rural, held-out, development) to a human for confirmation; at 8 h it never fires because no simulated failure lasts more than 4 h | a timeout chosen on evidence | needs field data (licence to be checked) |
